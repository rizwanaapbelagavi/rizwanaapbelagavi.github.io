📿 Daily Amal & Wazaif

Rozana ke Qurani Duayein, Azkaar aur Wazaif

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَنِ الرَّحِيمِ

Allah Ta'ala ke zikr se dilon ko sukoon milta hai. Is page par kuch mashhoor Qurani duayein aur masnoon azkaar diye gaye hain jo rozana padhne ke liye yaad rakhne mein aasaan hain.

Niyyat, yaqeen aur pabandi ke saath dua karein.

🌅 Subah ke Amal

1. Ayat-ul-Kursi

اللَّهُ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْحَيُّ الْقَيُّومُ ۚ لَا تَأْخُذُهُ سِنَةٌ وَلَا نَوْمٌ

Ayat-ul-Kursi Qur'an ki Surah Al-Baqarah, Ayat 255 hai. Iski poori tilawat karein.

📖 Reference: Qur'an 2:255
⏰ Subah aur shaam padhna achha amal hai.

2. Surah Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq & An-Naas

Subah aur shaam Surah Al-Ikhlas, Surah Al-Falaq aur Surah An-Naas ki tilawat karein.

📖 Reference: Masnoon morning/evening adhkar. In Surahs ki fazilat aur subah-shaam padhne ki riwayat ahadith mein milti hai.

🤲 Rozana Padhne Wali Duayein

3. Istighfar

أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ وَأَتُوبُ إِلَيْهِ

Astaghfirullaha wa atoobu ilaih.

Meaning: Main Allah se maghfirat maangta hoon aur Usi ki taraf tauba karta hoon.

🤲 Rozana jitna aasaan ho istighfar karein.

4. Pareshani ke Waqt ki Dua

لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ سُبْحَانَكَ إِنِّي كُنْتُ مِنَ الظَّالِمِينَ

La ilaha illa anta subhanaka inni kuntu minaz-zalimin.

Meaning: Aye Allah! Tere siwa koi mabood nahi. Tu paak hai. Beshak mujhse ghalati hui.

📖 Reference: Qur'an 21:87

5. Duniya aur Aakhirat ki Bhalai ki Dua

رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ

Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanatan wa fil-akhirati hasanatan wa qina azaban-naar.

Meaning: Aye hamare Rabb! Humein duniya mein bhi bhalai ata farma aur aakhirat mein bhi bhalai ata farma, aur humein Jahannum ke azaab se bacha.

📖 Reference: Qur'an 2:201

🕌 Namaz ke Baad

6. Tasbeeh, Tahmeed & Takbeer

Namaz ke baad Allah ka zikr karein:

SubhanAllah — Allah paak hai.
Alhamdulillah — Saari tareef Allah ke liye hai.
Allahu Akbar — Allah sabse bada hai.

🤲 Namaz ke baad masnoon azkaar ki pabandi karein.

🌙 Shaam ke Amal

7. Allah ka Zikr

لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ

La ilaha illallah.

Meaning: Allah ke siwa koi mabood nahi.

❤️ Dil ko Allah ke zikr se jodne ki koshish karein.

🛡️ Hifazat ke Liye

8. Mu'awwidhat

Surah Al-Ikhlas, Surah Al-Falaq aur Surah An-Naas ki tilawat karein aur Allah se hifazat ki dua karein.

📖 Reference: Qur'an 112, 113 & 114

💰 Rizq & Barkat

9. Istighfar aur Dua

رَبِّ إِنِّي لِمَا أَنْزَلْتَ إِلَيَّ مِنْ خَيْرٍ فَقِيرٌ

Rabbi inni lima anzalta ilayya min khairin faqeer.

Meaning: Aye mere Rabb! Jo bhi bhalai Tu meri taraf nazil kare, main uska mohtaj hoon.

📖 Reference: Qur'an 28:24

😌 Sukoon-e-Dil

10. Allah ka Zikr

أَلَا بِذِكْرِ اللَّهِ تَطْمَئِنُّ الْقُلُوبُ

Ala bi zikrillahi tatma'innul quloob.

Meaning: Beshak Allah ke zikr se dilon ko itminan aur sukoon milta hai.

📖 Reference: Qur'an 13:28

🌙 Sone se Pehle

11. Ayat-ul-Kursi & Qurani Tilawat

Sone se pehle Ayat-ul-Kursi aur Mu'awwidhat ki tilawat karke Allah se hifazat aur aman ki dua karein.

🤲 Apne ghar walon ke liye bhi dua karein.
